Customer Segmentation

Targeted Marketing via K-Means Clustering

ML K-Means Clustering

Project Overview

Implemented an unsupervised machine learning model to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior and demographic profiles. This allows for highly targeted marketing campaigns and personalized product recommendations.

Clustering Strategy

Cluster 1: High Spenders

Premium customers with high frequency and high order value.

Cluster 2: Bargain Hunters

Seasonal shoppers primarily active during sales and events.

Cluster 3: Occasional Shoppers

Low frequency but loyal customers for specific categories.

Technical Depth

Used the **Elbow Method** to determine the optimal number of clusters (K=5). Performed Principal Component Analysis (PCA) for dimensionality reduction before clustering.
