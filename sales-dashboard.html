Designed a comprehensive Power BI dashboard for 'Madhav Store' to track real-time sales performance. The dashboard enables stakeholders to drill down into regional data, payment method preferences, and monthly growth trends.

Dashboard Features

  • 📊 **Dynamic Slicers**: Filter by Month, State, and Category.
  • 📈 **KPI Tracking**: Real-time monitoring of Profit, Quantity, and AOV.
  • 🗺️ **Map Visuals**: Geographic distribution of sales volume.
  • 💳 **Payment Analysis**: Trend analysis for COD, UPI, and Credit Cards.
